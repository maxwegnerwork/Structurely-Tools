🔍 RevOps Insights — What the Data Tells You
📞
Call-first drives faster, higher connection
Campaign A connects 21.3% of leads vs 18.4% for B, and does it in 4.2 attempts vs 5.1. For Zillow leads this indicates immediate call intent outperforms text nurture. The higher opt-out rate (3.8%) is a trade-off worth monitoring.
A wins on volume + speed
💬
Text-first produces better lead experience
Campaign B's opt-out rate is 2.1% vs 3.8% — significantly below the 3% threshold. Leads in B who do connect via text are warmer and less likely to feel cold-called. Consider B for channels where lead quality is lower or more relationship-sensitive.
B wins on opt-out + text conversions
💡
Recommendation
Scale Campaign A for Zillow (call-ready intent). Consider a hybrid: calls for attempts 1–3, then text for 4–6 if no connect. Email remains low-yield at both positions — consider replacing later email attempts with additional call or text touches.
