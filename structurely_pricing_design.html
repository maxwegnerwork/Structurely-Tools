Frequently Asked Questions
How does usage-based pricing work?
Your monthly platform fee covers access — the platform, your agents, and all integrations. On top of that, you pay a per-credit rate only for what the AI actually does. If your AI isn't working leads, you're not spending credits. No minimums, no idle charges, no wasted budget.
What is an Action Credit?
An Action Credit is a unit of AI labor. 1 credit = 1 SMS response, 10 seconds of AI talk time, or 2 emails. Credits are consumed as your AI agents work — following up with leads, qualifying borrowers, and initiating transfers. You only pay for actual work performed.
Is there a cap on how many leads I can run?
No cap on any plan. Your platform fee gives you unlimited access — the only variable cost is your per-credit rate based on how much the AI actually works those leads. Run 100 leads or 100,000 — the platform supports it.
What's included in the onboarding fee?
Onboarding is a one-time fee that varies by plan: $1,500 for Individual, $2,000 for Team, and $2,500 for Company. All tiers include platform setup, agents configured for your vertical, local area code number setup, and a dedicated Account Manager for 60 days.
What are typical transfer rates?
Transfer rates vary by lead type and speed-to-lead. For warm leads (LendingTree, NerdWallet, etc.), clients see 5–30% live transfer rates. For colder leads (credit triggers, data leads), transfer rates average 0.5–3%. Your dedicated Account Manager works with you to optimize over the first 60 days.
What is the AI Power Dialer?
The AI Power Dialer is a Salesforce-native add-on that lets you toggle between human dialing and AI dialing per campaign. In AI mode it dials your full list simultaneously and transfers qualified leads live to your LOs. Available as an add-on for $999/mo base with additional seats at $199/mo. Currently in Beta.
